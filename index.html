05Why Bulgaria

The smart move - from abroad or at home.

Bulgaria's entry into the Eurozone opens a new era of stability and growth. Attractive prices, low taxes and rising rental demand make Sofia and the wider country one of Europe's most promising property markets - for international buyers and Bulgarians alike.

Eurozone Member

Added stability, currency confidence, and access to the deep European capital market.

Growing Economy

Steady GDP expansion, rising incomes, and one of the EU's lowest debt-to-GDP ratios.

Attractive Prices

Lower entry costs than Western Europe with equivalent build quality in prime areas.

Low Taxes

10% flat corporate and personal income tax - among the lowest in the European Union.

Rental Demand

Gross yields of 5–8% in major cities and 8–12% in coastal and ski markets.

Strategic Location

Bridging the EU, the Balkans, the Black Sea and Asia - with fast, affordable flights.

06Frequently Asked

Answers, before you ask.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly →
Can foreigners buy property in Bulgaria?
Yes. EU citizens buy directly. Non-EU citizens can buy apartments and buildings freely, and land via a Bulgarian company we can set up for you.
Do you only work with investors, or can I buy a home to live in?
Both, equally. Whether you're building a rental portfolio or relocating your family, the service is the same end-to-end process - sourcing, construction, design and furnishing. The only difference is what you do with the keys at the end.
What is the typical rental yield?
Gross rental yields typically range from 5–8% in major cities and 8–12% in tourism-driven coastal and ski markets.
Do you manage the property after purchase?
Yes - renovation, furnishing, tenant sourcing, rent collection, maintenance, and reporting. A single monthly statement.
How long does a typical purchase take?
From accepted offer to keys typically takes 30–60 days including due diligence and notary formalities.
What are the total transaction costs?
Typically 4–5% of the purchase price covering notary, municipal tax, registration, and legal fees.
Do you handle turnkey renovation, or do I need to hire contractors myself?
Turnkey is the whole point. We manage architects, licensed contractors and site supervision from the first wall opened to the final photo - you approve decisions, we run the work. No separate contractors to hire or manage.
What's the difference between the Essential, Signature and Bespoke finish levels?
Essential is an efficient, rental-ready finish for buy-to-let budgets. Signature adds premium materials and a designed interior throughout. Bespoke is fully custom architecture, joinery and furniture for owner-occupiers or luxury lets. The process is identical - only the finish level and cost change.
Do you offer interior design and furnishing, or only construction?
Both, as one service. Every renovation includes interior design - layout, materials, lighting and a bespoke furniture package - so the property is delivered styled and ready to list or move into, not just built.
Can you source premium or luxury properties, not just renovate them?
Yes. Property sourcing is the first step of the service, and includes premium and luxury listings - as well as off-market opportunities - in Sofia, Plovdiv, Varna, Bansko and Sozopol, matched to your budget and investment goals.
How much does a full renovation and furnishing package cost?
Renovation and furnishing budgets typically range from Essential buy-to-let finishes to fully bespoke luxury execution, scoped to the property and finish level after a walkthrough. Every quote is itemized before work begins, with no hidden costs.
Do I need to be in Bulgaria during the renovation or design process?
No. Most clients manage the entire process - design approvals, material selections and progress updates - remotely, with photo and video updates at each milestone. Many never visit the property until it's finished.
